WHY CHOOSE US

Why Choose Woodland Institute Of Nursing

Choosing the right nursing college can shape your career in health care — and Woodland Institute of Nursing stands out as a strong choice for aspiring nurses who want a solid foundation in both theory and practice.

1. Established Legacy and Commitment
Woodland Institute of Nursing was established in 2006 with the mission to develop skilled nursing professionals who can meet the growing healthcare needs of the country. The institute focuses on producing leaders not only in clinical settings but also in academic and administrative roles, preparing students to take on diverse responsibilities in the healthcare sector.

2. Comprehensive Accredited Programs
The institute offers nursing courses recognized by the Indian Nursing Council.

  • General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) – a foundational 3-year diploma for hands-on nursing practice.
  • Basic B.Sc. Nursing – a 4-year degree that builds deep competence in advanced nursing care.
  • Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing – caters to working nurses who want to further their skills and qualifications.

All programs combine scientific knowledge with practical clinical experience, preparing students to provide high-quality care in hospitals and community settings.

3. Strong Academic Foundations
The curriculum is built on a robust theoretical base, integrated with real-world clinical training. Students learn not just nursing techniques, but also critical thinking, communication skills, ethics, leadership, and community health — all essential for modern nursing professionals.

4. Experienced Faculty and Supportive Environment
The institute is staffed by qualified and dedicated nursing educators who guide students through their studies and clinical practice. This mentorship fosters both professional confidence and personal growth, helping students become compassionate caregivers and effective health team members.

5. Peaceful, Enriching Campus Setting
Located in Laitkor, Shillong, the campus offers a serene and green environment — perfect for focused study and personal well-being. Surrounded by hills, fresh air, and natural beauty, students benefit from a peaceful atmosphere alongside a strong academic culture.

6. Preparation for Real-World Nursing Careers
With clinical exposure in healthcare settings and an emphasis on hands-on training, Woodland prepares graduates to enter the workforce confidently. Whether locally or globally, alumni are equipped with the skills to make meaningful contributions to patient care and public health.

Our Institute

Woodland Institute of Nursing

Woodland Institute of Nursing, under the umbrella of Woodland Hospital, Shillong, is a reputed private nursing institution committed to excellence in nursing education.

The institute is established under the aegis of the North East Hub Society (NEHUBS), a registered Non-Government Organization dedicated to educational and social development in the North Eastern region of India. Located in the serene city of Shillong, Meghalaya, the institute provides an ideal environment for focused learning and personal growth.

Woodland Institute of Nursing is duly recognized by the Meghalaya Nursing Council (MNC) and the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi, ensuring that all academic programs meet national standards of nursing education.

The institute is affiliated with North Eastern Hill University (NEHU), providing strong academic credibility to its programs.

Students benefit from extensive hands-on clinical exposure at Woodland Hospital, allowing them to apply classroom knowledge in real healthcare environments and build confidence in patient care.

  • Recognized by Indian Nursing Council (INC) & Meghalaya Nursing Council (MNC)
  • Affiliated with North Eastern Hill University (NEHU)
  • Extensive hospital-based clinical training
  • Modern skill labs and simulation-based learning
  • Highly experienced and supportive faculty
  • Peaceful and student-friendly campus environment
