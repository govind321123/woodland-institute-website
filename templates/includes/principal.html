Our foremost challenge in health care today is the shortage of qualified nurses in both clinical practice and nursing education. To overcome this challenge Woodland Institute of Nursing had started with a GNM program for students desirous of developing skills and competency in nursing. In a span of 4 years the Basic B.Sc. and Post Basic B.Sc Nursing programs were opened in the newly constructed building at the Laitkor Campus. Over a period of 6 years, we have about 300 students who have graduated from the Institute in various programs and working in different areas of Health Organization.
